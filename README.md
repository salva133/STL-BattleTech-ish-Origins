# BattleTech-ish Origins

A Stellaris mod inspired by the BattleTech universe. Adds two new origins that spawn a fully functional federation — laid out like the Inner Sphere — at game start.

**Requires:** Stellaris 4.3.7 (Cetus) · Federations DLC

---

## Origins

### Clan Confederation
*Inspired by the BattleTech Clans.*

Your empire leads a confederation of six warrior clans, forming a **Hegemony Federation** with you as permanent leader. The clans dwell in a sealed cluster beyond the galactic rim, reachable only through a gateway at your home — their staging ground for the invasion to come. Your species carries the mark of generations of selective breeding: the Trueborn trait grants +2 genetic points and +10% army damage. Every clan is of your own species, holds a small home territory, and starts at maximum cohesion with relations between all members locked at their peak.

- Requires: `civic_warrior_culture`, non-Imperial authority, biological species
- Federation: Hegemony, 600 XP, 100 Cohesion, 20-year succession law
- Partners: 6 clans beyond the rim (7 members total)

### Stellar Union
*Inspired by the Star League.*

Your empire stands at the centre of a **Star League-style federation** — the heart of the Inner Sphere. You are the centre, and five great houses ring around your throne. All are of your own species, each holding a small home territory, beginning at full cohesion with relations between every member at their maximum.

- Requires: `auth_imperial`, Militarist or Fanatic Militarist, non-Spiritualist, biological species
- Federation: Hegemony, player as leader
- Partners: 5 great houses (6 members total)

---

## Rival Empire

Whichever origin you pick, a rival empire with the **opposing origin** spawns in a distant part of the galaxy at game start — a Stellar Union if you lead the Clans, a Clan Confederation if you lead the Union. The rival builds its own full federation independently: an Inner Sphere of its own, or a Clan cluster beyond the rim.

AI empires will almost never randomly roll these origins (weight 1:100,000).

---

## Installation

Place the mod folder into `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Stellaris/mod/` and enable it in the Stellaris launcher.
